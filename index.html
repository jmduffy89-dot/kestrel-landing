Hundreds of live notices publish every week. Almost none are worth your time.

Public procurement portals list everything, indiscriminately — highways, catering, IT, office furniture, all in the same feed. Kestrel filters that down to what's actually relevant to your business, automatically.

Real judgment, not keyword matching

Kestrel reads the real substance of every notice — scope, evaluation criteria, sector fit — the way a person would, not by matching words. Every judgment comes with its reasoning shown, so you can check it rather than simply trust it.

Nothing is submitted, chased, or flagged unless it genuinely fits your business.

One document, or the whole pack

Upload everything from the tender — Kestrel works out which document actually holds the real questions, word limits, and evaluation weighting, wherever they sit in the pack.

Grounded, never invented
