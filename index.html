How it works

Read everything. Judge properly. Draft when it counts.

Read

Every live notice, every 4 hours

Kestrel reads the whole public sector feed and cross-checks it against the per-notice records, so the notices the list quietly drops are recovered too.

Judge

Fit by judgment, not keywords

Scope, evaluation criteria and sector fit are weighed against your business, and the reasoning is shown. What it leaves out is kept, searchable and explained.

Draft

A grounded first response

It reads the whole tender pack, finds the questions, word limits and weightings, and drafts only from your own profile and past projects. Unknowns are flagged, not invented.

Pipeline

See what is coming, not just what is open.

Planned tenders and likely re-lets, spotted early from the notices buyers publish before they go to market. Watch one and you hear the moment it opens.
